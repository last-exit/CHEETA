This revision supersedes the body design in homepage-organization-plan-2026-11-08.md. Local only; no push or deployment.

The user rejected the layered arch, isolated gun and glasses because they looked pasted together. The final Gun feature uses one composed still-life scene per reference. The gun and Maharajah sit on separate stone ledges in the same ivory niche, with shared perspective, lighting and shadows. The scene contains exactly one pair. The heading and controls have their own space outside the picture. Typography is reduced from the rejected oversized overlapping layout.

All three references remain selectable with existing preload/decode, latest-request, retry, keyboard and reduced-motion handling. The chapter now changes complete photographic states; it does not place unrelated cutouts over a background. Existing product IDs, prices and cart behavior are untouched.

Masquerade no longer has a rose or text overlapping the model. Its title/copy and portrait occupy separate grid columns, then stack on phones. Retail reverses that composition. The Philosophy quote and ivory sculpture sit in separate blocks. The opening film and Icon livin’ presentation are preserved.
