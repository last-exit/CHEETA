### Portrait salon

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a NEW original companion architectural campaign photograph, using the provided image only as a material and lighting reference. This will sit behind a real founder portrait and live website typography in Cheeta Jewels' luxury vintage revival Philosophy page. A private neoclassical ivory salon, straight-on frontal eye-level view, wide landscape approximately 3:2. Champagne gilt, worn chalk-white plaster and carved acanthus, beautiful soft daylight and deep but delicate architectural shadows, expensive European antique interior with restraint. COMPOSITION IMPORTANT: the left 52% is a large quiet pale ivory wall with only very faint window shadows and a little moulding around its outer edge, clean enough for dark text. Right 48% has a tall recessed arched wall niche, its interior plain ivory for an oval portrait which will be added by code. Place a narrow ornate carved ivory console UNDER the niche in lower right at around 84% image height, its large curved legs partly visible. A SINGLE muted champagne-gold tassel hanging near far right edge hints at vintage upholstery just outside crop. Upper margin is plain warm ivory like #f7f3ec, architectural detail gathers around the right niche and bottom. No portrait, no face, no picture frame, no art inside niche, no statue, no diamond, no cheetah, no glasses, no film screen, no lettering, no logo. Not a wallpaper pattern, not flat vector shapes, not faux web UI. Photographic material depth, understated slightly aged workmanship, no cracks or grain overlays, no modern furniture. Design the console and niche so a separate portrait can appear genuinely hung on this wall. The reference's hero scene, cheetah sculpture and pedestal must NOT be copied; this is a different view in the same visual world.

### Film surround

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: transparent decorative architectural surround for a real video on a vintage luxury jewellery website. Create one exquisitely carved antique IVORY and pale stone rectangular architectural overmantel, seen perfectly straight-on, a single self-contained physical carved object floating on TRUE TRANSPARENCY. Width to height approximately 3:2, elegant generous horizontal shape. The central opening must be a clean empty TRANSPARENT RECTANGLE with square corners, approximately 85% of outer width and 80% of outer height, aspect ratio about 17:12. All inside and outside of the carved surround is fully transparent alpha, no filler, no checkerboard, no black panel. Very shallow pale champagne gilt on tips of acanthus leaves, warm almost-white chalk paint, authentic antique workmanship. Thin side uprights and lower ledge, slightly fuller upper cornice, a beautiful sculpted small central crest of acanthus leaves at the top, no crest insignia or crown. Low relief side leaves and restrained beading, real photographed carving with soft delicate daylight from upper left. The RECTANGULAR OPENING remains unobstructed and truly empty; nothing crosses its inner edges. No curved arch over the opening; top outer cornice may have graceful flourishes. No glass, no image, no room, no fireplace, no screen, no text, no logos, no humans. No florid gilded rococo gold block; mostly ivory, refined and expensive. Keep the object sharp with its actual small recess shadows, not a cast drop shadow around its outer perimeter. Outer silhouette completely visible with a little transparent margin.
