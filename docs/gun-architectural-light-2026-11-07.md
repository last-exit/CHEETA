# Gun architectural light

Replaced the engraving experiment with a single soft, cusped palace-arch shadow across the warm-white page surface. The right side carries the impression while the copy remains clear on the left. Gun’s body and footer now reveal that same surface, including the next-collection area the client identified. Films and the collector’s case retain their own imagery.

The inline SVG uses two blurred shapes, remains static and is hidden from assistive technology. It accepts no pointer input, introduces no scroll handler or animation, and becomes lighter on phones. This is a decorative architectural suggestion, not a claim about the actual showroom. No print, particles, panels or divider lines were added. The founder imagery and engraving crop remain removed.

Checked the real footer at 1480 × 1034 and the standalone phone layout. No horizontal overflow. Screenshot: `../gun-architectural-light-review-2026-11-07/desktop-footer.png`. The prior implementation is preserved in that review directory. Shared cinema performance was subsequently improved without changing the accepted case interaction. All work remains local.
